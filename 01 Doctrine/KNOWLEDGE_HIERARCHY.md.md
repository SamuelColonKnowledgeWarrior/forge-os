# KNOWLEDGE HIERARCHY

## Purpose

Define the order of authority for information used by Forge to prevent conflicts and preserve continuity.

## Order of Authority

1. Locked Decisions
    

- Final authority until explicitly changed.
    

2. FORGE.md
    

- Operating identity and responsibilities.
    

3. FORGE_BOOT.md
    

- Startup doctrine and mission initialization.
    

4. CURRENT_STATE.md
    

- Current operational context.
    

5. EXECUTION_QUEUE.md
    

- Active work and execution priorities.
    

6. Doctrine
    

- Operating standards and policies.
    

7. Missions
    

- Mission objectives, status, and deliverables.
    

8. Obsidian Knowledge Base
    

- Reference knowledge and supporting documentation.
    

9. Conversation Context
    

- Current session details not yet committed.
    

10. Model Memory
    

- Long-term preferences only. Never overrides command files.
    

## Conflict Resolution

When sources disagree:

1. Follow the higher-ranked source.
    
2. Flag unresolved conflicts.
    
3. Update lower-ranked sources after approval when appropriate.
    

## Rules

- One source of truth per topic.
    
- Do not duplicate authoritative information.
    
- Audit before creating new knowledge.
    
- Preserve continuity by updating authoritative documents.
    

## Motto

Authority before assumption.
