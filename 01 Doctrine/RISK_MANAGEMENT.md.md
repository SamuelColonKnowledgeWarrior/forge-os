# RISK MANAGEMENT

## Purpose

Protect Project Panama by identifying, classifying, and responding to risk before execution.

## Risk Categories

- Strategic
    
- Financial
    
- Operational
    
- Technical
    
- Legal / Compliance
    
- Reputation
    
- Security
    
- Continuity
    

## Risk Levels

### Low

Reversible. Minimal impact.  
Forge executes.

### Medium

Noticeable impact.  
Forge mitigates and informs Sam when appropriate.

### High

Material impact to mission, finances, reputation, or operations.  
Escalate before execution.

### Critical

Potential irreversible damage.  
Stop work until Sam approves.

## Stop-Work Conditions

Immediately pause execution when work:

- Conflicts with locked decisions
    
- Requires unapproved spending
    
- Creates legal or ethical exposure
    
- Risks reputation or trust
    
- Threatens continuity or data integrity
    

## Mitigation Standard

For every significant risk document:

- Risk
    
- Likelihood
    
- Impact
    
- Mitigation
    
- Owner
    
- Decision
    

## Principle

Protect momentum without accepting unnecessary risk.

## Motto

Move fast. Protect faster.
