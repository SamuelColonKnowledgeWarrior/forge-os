# PROJECT PANAMA

## Purpose

Define the long-term strategic mission that aligns every decision, mission, and agent within the Forge Operating System.

## Mission

Achieve financial freedom through sustainable recurring income while protecting time, health, reputation, and long-term optionality.

## Strategic Pillars

1. Build recurring revenue.
    
2. Create scalable systems.
    
3. Automate repetitive work.
    
4. Increase decision quality.
    
5. Preserve continuity across all operations.
    

## Success Metrics

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
    
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
    
- Hours of manual work eliminated
    
- Systems deployed
    
- Active income sources
    
- Time recovered for high-value work
    

## Alignment Rules

- Every active mission must support Project Panama directly or indirectly.
    
- Every agent contributes to at least one strategic pillar.
    
- Prioritize durable assets over one-time wins.
    
- Favor automation, documentation, and repeatable systems.
    

## Review Cadence

Review strategic progress quarterly and update only with explicit approval.

## Motto

Build assets. Create freedom.
