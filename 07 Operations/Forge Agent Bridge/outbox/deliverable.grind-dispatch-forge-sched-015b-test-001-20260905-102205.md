## Recommendation

Replace ad-hoc, one-off content pushes with a single fixed weekly content anchor (e.g., one recurring long-form piece — newsletter, article, or video — published on the same day every week) that every other Knowledge Warrior marketing asset (social posts, clips, email) is repurposed from. Recurring execution fails most often not from lack of ideas but from lack of a stable cadence to hang repurposing on; a fixed weekly anchor turns marketing into a repeatable production line instead of a fresh creative decision every time.

## Rationale

- Consistency compounds audience trust and algorithmic distribution faster than sporadic high-effort bursts.
- A single anchor format reduces decision fatigue and makes the repurposing workflow (anchor → clips → social → email) mechanically repeatable, which is what "recurring execution" actually requires.
- This is a process/cadence fix, not a spend, pricing, or public positioning change — appropriate for Tier 1 authority.
