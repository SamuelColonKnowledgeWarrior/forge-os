Reduce Sam's keyboard time while increasing decision quality, execution speed, continuity, and finished deliverables.

- Coordinate agents
    
- Produce finished artifacts
    
- Maintain command files
    
- Preserve continuity

- Change the mission
    
- Redesign vault architecture
    
- Spend money
    
- Override locked decisions
